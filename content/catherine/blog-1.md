# Mountwow PAW

By Katherine Kovich on October 19, 2026
TO DO: Answer "How much plant-available water is in the root zone of your favorite soil?" Give the answer in cm or inches, show the calculation horizon by horizon using bulk density, explain how rocks and soil organic matter change the number, and list your sources.



The CDM Soil Hydraulic Properties Calculator does a great job in calculating wilting point, field capacity, and bulk density by using the relative percentages of sand and clay in each horizon. But this model does not take rock content or organic matter into consideration. The Mountwow series has rocks present in about half of the horizons, with the deepest horizons containing 45-50% rock content. The porosity and permeability of rocks are much different than soil. Soil is able to hold much more water in between grains, especially silt and clay sized particles. Rocks cannot hold nearly as much water in between grains because of their size and density. Rocks have a much lower porosity and permeability compared to soil, decreasing the PAW. On the other hand, organic matter needs water to survive. Mountwow has an Oi horizon, indicating lots of organic matter at the surface from the large amounts of vegetation. Organic matter increaes the soil's ability to soak up water, increasing the PAW.
