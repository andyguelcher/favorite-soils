This group is Amelie Vernooy, Andy Guelcher, and Katherine Kovich in lab section 8. Two of our soils, Wauseon and Mountwow, are wet for part of the year and the other, Weminuche, drains freely. They all have different acidities. Wauseon being entirely basic, Mountwow being entirely acidic, and Weminuche being somewhere between. 

Each soil has its own page with its DRoCTS+ table and water comparisons. Our plant-available water and annual water budget posts are on the [blog](blog.html).
