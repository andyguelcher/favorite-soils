In the event of the Weminuche soil being impacted by a drought, what I would do depends on what I was using the land for. If the soils were for wildlife and/or natural areas and intended to receive minimal aid, I would not interfere. If I was using the soil for timber, however, I might turn to drip tubing or soaker hoses to provide the soil with its typical amount of water.
